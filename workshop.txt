title=Better Workshop
description=[h1]Description[/h1]
description=Better Workshop replaces the main menu Workshop screen. Instead of a wizard of separate pages, all your Workshop items are on one screen: pick an item on the left, edit and upload it on the right. The original wizard is still one click away.
description=
description=[h1]Features[/h1]
description=[list]
description=    [*]All your Workshop items in one searchable list, with their thumbnail, Workshop ID and status
description=    [*]Problems show before you upload: missing preview.png or mod.info, wrong preview size, blocked file types, missing title or description
description=    [*]Title, visibility, description, tags and change notes on one screen. Save writes workshop.txt without uploading
description=    [*]Description and change notes preview with Steam formatting: headings, lists, bold text and links
description=    [*]Full-size editor for long descriptions
description=    [*]Lists the mods inside each item with their ID, version and minimum build
description=    [*]Shows when a published item was last updated on Steam, and its size
description=    [*]Link an unpublished folder to an existing Workshop item by pasting its ID
description=    [*]Upload progress as a checklist, with percentage and the Steam log
description=    [*]Copy an item's ID, open its folder, or open the Workshop and your items in the Steam overlay
description=    [*]The [b]Classic[/b] button opens the original vanilla wizard
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]How to use[/h1]
description=Enable Better Workshop in the Mods screen of the main menu, then open [b]Workshop[/b] from the main menu.
description=
description=[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
description=[list]
description=    [*][b]Other Mods[/b] - Conflicts with other mods that replace the main menu Workshop screen
description=    [*][b]Game Version[/b] - Build 42 only
description=    [*][b]Multiplayer[/b] - Main menu only, does not affect gameplay or servers
description=    [*][b]Pre-existing saves[/b] - Does not touch saves. Safe to add or remove at any time
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Known Bugs/Issues[/h1]
description=[list]
